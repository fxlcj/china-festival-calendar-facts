# Known gaps and deliberate exclusions

This file is **not a dataset**. Nothing here is a verified fact and nothing here may be quoted as one. It records the dates we wanted to include but could not verify against an official source, together with entries we removed because the evidence did not hold. It exists so that a later maintainer does not quietly fill these holes with a guess.

| Missing or excluded | Why it is not in `facts.json` | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 515 simplified characters and 54 simplified radicals in the 1956 Chinese Character Simplification Scheme | The official text we found is 1956 issue No. 6 of the State Council Bulletin, published as scanned images with no text layer. Its figures cannot be checked line by line against a claim. | Quality pass, 2026-11-11; see the registry entry for that date. |
| Precise start times of the 24 solar terms for 2026 and 2027 | Entries sourced from a Purple Mountain Observatory PDF were dropped: the times in the PDF could not be matched line by line to the claims, and the observatory site refused automated access. A date we cannot re-check is not published. | Quality pass, 2026-11-11, 36 to 31 entries. |
| Holiday dates sourced from local government reposts | Those pages are copies, not the issuing authority. Holiday dates in this repo cite the gov.cn notice itself. | Quality pass, 2026-11-11. |
| Holiday arrangements for 2028 and later | No State Council notice has been issued. We do not extrapolate from earlier years. | Repo scope, checked 2026-11-11. |

If an official page becomes available, add the entry through the monthly maintenance task with its official source URL and check date, record the change in `CHANGELOG.md`, and remove the row above. The official page, not this file and not a third-party mirror, is always the authority.
